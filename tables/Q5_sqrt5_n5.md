[All rings](../README.md) · the same data: [`k_theory.json`](../data/k_theory.json), [`k_theory.csv`](../data/k_theory.csv)

The p-adic K-groups K_r(R; Z₅) for 1 ≤ r ≤ 100. As in [AKN, Appendix A], each entry lists the exponents a₁ ≤ a₂ ≤ … of the decomposition K_r(R; Z₅) ≅ Z/5^a₁ ⊕ Z/5^a₂ ⊕ …, and `0` is the zero group (a blank cell in [AKN]). K_0(R; Z₅) = Z₅.

K = Q₅(√5) = Q₅(√−5) is ramified over Q₅, with e = 2 and discriminant exponent 1 (LMFDB [5.1.2.1a1.1](https://www.lmfdb.org/padicField/5.1.2.1a1.1)). R = O_K/ϖ⁵ = Z₅[z]/(E(z), z⁵) with E(z) = z² + 5, the defining polynomial of the LMFDB, and ϖ a root of E. |R| = 5⁵.

[AKN] tabulate no ring of this kind, so all 100 groups are new relative to their tables. They are in **bold**.
